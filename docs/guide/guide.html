01

Install & update

Administrator
  1. Download VIDA-Dental-Setup-x.y.z.exe from the VIDA release page and open it.
  2. If Windows says “protected your PC”: More info → Run anyway.
  3. To update: click the version number in the top bar → Check for updates → Download → Restart & install.
  4. No internet? Copy the new installer to a USB drive → Choose installer file…

TipUpdating never touches patient data. The app also checks for updates by itself a few seconds after it opens.

02

Before the clinic

Administrator
  1. First launch: type your name and choose a 4–6 digit PIN.
  2. Clinic events → + New clinic. Add name, trip code, community, country, dates, target.
  3. Leave “Start this clinic now” ticked. The top bar turns green.
  4. Staff → + Add person for each volunteer: name, role, PIN.
VIDA Dental · Quick guide4
03

Sign in & practise

Everyone
  1. Tap your name, then type your PIN.
  2. First time? Press Start walkthrough: a hands-on practice of registering a patient. Nothing is saved.
  3. Do what the orange box asks to move on. Replay any time with the ? button.

04

Register a patient · 1/3

Registration
  1. Pick the patient’s language: Español or English.
  2. Search first: name, birth date or phone. Found? Press Start visit.
  3. Not found → New patient. Fill every field (birth date as DD/MM/YYYY).
VIDA Dental · Quick guide6
04

Register a patient · 2/3

Registration
  1. Read each question aloud (or press the speaker) and tap Sí or No. A red edge means a warning for the dentist.
  2. Takes medicines? Tap them in the list.
  3. Consent: read it aloud, tick both boxes, the patient signs with a finger or mouse.

04

Register a patient · 3/3

Registration
  1. Under 18: a parent or guardian signs and picks their relationship.
  2. Press Sign and register.
  3. Give the patient their number. They wait to be called for triage.
VIDA Dental · Quick guide8
05

Triage

Dentist
  1. Tap the next patient in line (the next five are shown).
  2. Check the red warnings and health summary.
  3. Add complaint, blood pressure, pulse, priority.
  4. Pick a mark (or press C, F, X, S), then tap or drag across teeth. Ctrl+Z undoes.
  5. Choose where they go: Cleaning, Restorative, or Exam only.
  6. Press Save and send on.
VIDA Dental · Quick guide9
06

Education & treatment

Assistants · Dentists
  1. Education: tick the topics taught → Done — send to treatment.
  2. Treatment: pick the tab, Cleaning or Restorative, then the patient.
  3. Record what was done (rows are pre-filled from triage) → Mark … done.
VIDA Dental · Quick guide10
07

Checkout

Coordinator
  1. Open a patient under Ready for checkout.
  2. Read the after-care aloud (pre-ticked).
  3. Add prescriptions from the pharmacy list.
  4. Tick follow-up if they need care next trip.
  5. Hand over the screen for the 1–5 survey.
  6. Press Check out.
VIDA Dental · Quick guide11
08

Dashboard & patient records

Everyone
  1. Dashboard: today’s counts, progress to target, and where every patient is.
  2. Patients: search any past clinic. See visits, treatments and follow-ups.
  3. Press Start a new visit to register a returning patient.

09

End of the day

Administrator
  1. Settings & backup → Back up now… → save the file.
  2. Copy that file to VIDA’s Google Drive.
  3. Last day of the trip: Clinic events → Finish. Records are kept.

TipThe app also saves its own copy every hour and keeps one per day for 30 days.

10

Questions & answers

Do we need internet?

No. Everything works offline. Internet is only used to check for updates.

Someone forgot their PIN.

An administrator opens Staff → Set PIN.

Wrong PIN too many times?

After 5 tries the account waits one minute.

A patient came before.

Search on Registration or Patients. Their history and follow-ups appear.

“Consent missing” is shown.

Tap that tag on the patient to take the consent. No care until it is signed.

Read aloud makes no sound.

Add a Spanish voice in Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech. Or read it yourself.

A patient left early.

Checkout → open them → Left without care.

I need to fix a checked-out visit.

An administrator opens Patients → the visit → Re-open to correct.

Need more room on screen?

Fold the patient list with the panel icon next to the station name.

How do I change tooth numbering?

Settings & backup → FDI (11–48) or Universal (1–32).

The laptop broke.

Install on another laptop → Settings & backup → Restore from a backup…

Who saw a record?

Administrators: Audit log lists every view and change.

Before live clinicsThe consent wording in this version is a draft. Replace it with VIDA’s approved text.
