# VIDA Dental — roadmap

Dates follow VIDA's intake: a working test version by **December 10**, the first live clinic on **December 16**, and 20 clinics between **December 14 and 27**.

| Release | Contents | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | Setup, PIN sign-in, staff, clinic events, Spanish/English check-in with medical history, warning flags, consent read aloud with signatures (guardian under 18), queue numbers, returning-patient search, triage (next five), education, cleaning and restorative stations with tooth chart and treatment checklist, checkout with after-care, allergy-checked prescriptions, follow-up and survey, patient records, audit log, backup/restore, online + USB updates | **Built** |
| 0.2 | Reports by year, season, country, trip code and community (patient counts, treatments, survey results, conditions, demographics, patients per dentist, progress against target), exported as PDF, Excel and CSV | Next |
| 0.3 | X-ray upload (images exported from the Woodpecker unit) attached to the visit; patient visit summary PDF to share by WhatsApp or email | Planned |
| 0.4 | Tablets and phones: one laptop hosts the clinic on local Wi-Fi so Android tablets and iPads join through their browser, with no internet needed (QR check-in, device-to-device sharing) | Planned |
| 0.5 | Voice-to-text notes; replace draft consent and form text with VIDA's approved forms in every language; VIDA logo | Waiting on VIDA's materials |
| Later | Cloud backup to VIDA's Google Workspace; medical and vision workflows | To scope |
