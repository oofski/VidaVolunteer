## VIDA Dental 0.1

The first test build of VIDA Dental — offline dental clinic software for VIDA (Vida Volunteer) clinics.

**Download:** `VIDA-Dental-Setup-<version>.exe` (installs, and updates itself online) or `VIDA-Dental-Portable-<version>.exe` (runs without installing; update it from a USB drive or folder).

### What's in this build
- First-run setup creates the administrator. Everyone signs in with their name and a short PIN.
- Clinic events with trip code, country, community, dates, season and a target patient count.
- Check-in in Spanish or English: registration, required yes/no medical history with warning flags, medication picker, consent read aloud, signature (drawn or created from the name), parent or guardian signature for patients under 18, and a queue number.
- Returning patients are found across every past clinic by name, date of birth, phone or community. Accents are ignored.
- Triage (the next five in line), education, cleaning and restorative / extraction stations with a tooth chart (FDI or Universal), and checkout with after-care, prescriptions checked against allergies, follow-up and the patient survey.
- Patient records across all trips, an audit log of who viewed or changed records, backup and restore, and hourly automatic backups.
- Software updates: online from GitHub, or from an installer on a USB drive.

### Before live clinics
- The consent text is **draft wording**. Replace it with VIDA's approved forms.
- The app icon is a placeholder until the VIDA logo arrives.
- Windows shows an "Unknown publisher" warning the first time, because the installer is not code-signed yet. Choose **More info → Run anyway**.
