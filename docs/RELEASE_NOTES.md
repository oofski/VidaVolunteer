## VIDA Dental 0.2

**Download:** `VIDA-Dental-Setup-<version>.exe` (installs, and updates itself online) or `VIDA-Dental-Portable-<version>.exe` (runs without installing; update it from a USB drive or folder).

### New in 0.2
- **Friendlier, faster tooth chart** (Triage and Treatment):
  - Real tooth shapes, coloured by what's marked.
  - Rounded mark chips that show their counts.
  - **Drag across several teeth** to mark them in one stroke.
  - Keys **C F X S M R** pick the mark, and **Ctrl+Z** undoes.
  - A **Charted** list beside the chart, with one-tap remove.
  - Right-click (or hold on a tablet) to add a note.
- **Registration walkthrough.** The first time each person signs in, they're offered a guided, hands-on practice of registering a patient.
  - It highlights one control at a time and waits for them to use it.
  - Steps covered: language, searching for returning patients, details, medical history, read-aloud consent, signature and queue number.
  - Practice mode uses an example patient and saves nothing.
  - Replay it any time from the **?** button in the header, or from **Show me how** on the Registration screen.
- **"Check-in" is now "Registration"**, with a side panel that shows where you are in the five steps and what to do next.
- **New industrial look**, built on IBM's Carbon Design System:
  - Square edges and IBM Plex type.
  - A dark header with VIDA orange used only for primary actions and the current item.
  - Carbon icons instead of symbols.
  - Fonts and icons are bundled, so it all works offline.
- **More room to work:**
  - The wide left sidebar is replaced by a slim icon rail. The menu button shows the labels.
  - The clinic name, offline status, version and your account move to the header.
  - Each station's queue can be folded away, giving the patient's chart the full width.

### Still in this build
Spanish/English registration with required medical history and warning flags, read-aloud consent with signatures (guardian under 18), returning-patient search, triage, education, cleaning and restorative stations with tooth chart, checkout with allergy-checked prescriptions, follow-up and survey, patient records, audit log, backup/restore, and online + USB updates.

### Before live clinics
- The consent text is **draft wording**. Replace it with VIDA's approved forms.
- The app icon is a placeholder until the VIDA logo arrives.
- Windows shows an "Unknown publisher" warning the first time, because the installer is not code-signed yet. Choose **More info → Run anyway**.
